Strategies  /  01

Fundamental positions on both sides of the book.

A concentrated, research-led book of long and short positions in public equities, built from company-level work rather than factor exposure or index construction.

Market
Public equities
Horizon
12–36 months
Construction
Concentrated, hedged

Our approach

01.1

01

Bottom-up first

Positions begin with the business — unit economics, capital intensity, incentives, and what the reported numbers do and do not capture. Sector views follow from company work, not the reverse.

02

Shorts as research, not insurance

The short book is underwritten to the same standard as the long book. We short businesses we believe are mispriced, not indices we want to neutralise.

03

Sized to conviction

Position size reflects the strength of the evidence and the cost of being wrong, not the comfort of a benchmark weight.

Process

01.2

01

Screening

Quantitative and qualitative screens surface candidates where price action and fundamentals have separated.

02

Primary research

Filings, channel work, competitor and customer conversations, and an explicit written thesis with falsifiable claims.

03

Underwriting

A base, upside and downside case, with the specific evidence that would force us to change our minds.

04

Sizing and entry

Initial size set against downside, scaled as the thesis confirms.

05

Monitoring

Quarterly re-underwriting against the original thesis. Drift in the reason we own something is treated as a sell signal.

Risk

01.3

Risk is managed where it originates.

Gross and net exposure are managed against the opportunity set rather than a fixed target. Single-name limits, liquidity screens and correlation review sit alongside the fundamental work. The dominant risk in a concentrated book is being wrong on the business, so most of the risk process is research discipline rather than statistical overlay.
